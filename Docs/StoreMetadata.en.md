Build your edit around scenes, then refine its timing on a timeline. Prepare portrait, square and landscape versions of the same project, with framing for each format.

Import your media, edit text and captions, rearrange your story and keep projects locally. Original templates and a sample project help you explore the workspace without an account. Export videos and carry your projects with their media to continue editing.

Microphone recording and system voices help you prepare narration. Transcription requires a compatible language, device and installed local resources. It does not fall back to cloud recognition.
